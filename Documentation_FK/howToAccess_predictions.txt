Small description of the output of our model and specifically on how to access each element of an horizon of prediction.

results_ = results[basin]["1h"]["xr"]
obs = results_['streamflow_obs']
sim = results_['streamflow_sim']

Organization of the files:
- for each date, a sequence of N points is produced (time step between points determined by the frequency of the dataset). N is the value of the parameter 'predict_last_n'.
- As an example: The data produced on the 2nd day of March 2020 at 2000, with a prediction over the last 24h would contains predicted value from the 1st March 2020 at 2100 until the 2nd March 2020 at 2000 (value included).


Access the data:
examples:
	obs.iloc[-1][-3] -> Data from last date, at t-3h (prediction at t+21h if horizon is 24h)
	obs.iloc[-1,-4] Gives the same value. Numbering is from t-0 to t-23h. Thus the -4 el of the list is t-3h (-1 el being the t-0h value thus the prediction at +24h)
	In the first case, we access the value under the time stamp (index name 'time_step') of -3h with respect to 't' that would be the last time stamp of prediction (construction of the sequence form NH)
	In the second case we access the 4th last element of a list

	Consequence: warning about the order of the square brackets here, it matters!
